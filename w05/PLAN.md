## Idea C (chosen): "The Aurora Peak" (find sounds in 360, then follow a giant with your eyes)
1. Night. You stand on top of a needle-thin stone peak, 300 m above a sea of clouds. Tiny Tink the bell-bird sits on the old bell post next to you.
2. Vela, a sky serpent made of aurora light (200 m long), loops right around your peak and away. She lost her song.
3. Crystal bells on the peaks all around you (in front, behind, far below, high above) ring one by one. Turn to the sound and look at the bell to answer it.
4. A storm rises from the clouds toward your feet; bells ring faster, two at once.
5. Climax: Vela bursts up out of the storm right in front of you, shoots up into the sky, spirals around your peak - follow her with your eyes - then stops face to face and you reach out to touch her nose. The whole sky turns to aurora.

## The test
1. Flat phone game? No. The bells ring from directions you can't see (behind, below, above): you find them only by turning your body to the sound. The serpent spirals around you 360 degrees and under your feet; the drop is under you.
2. Body action: turn to a sound / look down / look up / follow with your eyes / reach out (select) to touch.
3. Whoa (0-15 s): look down: 300 m of empty air under your feet. Then a song from behind and a 200 m glowing serpent wraps around your tiny peak and her huge face passes 8 m from you.
4. Reaction: Tink follows your eyes, hops and chirps when you look at him, flies toward a bell you miss. Vela's eyes always follow your head; while you follow her she sings and flies, if you lose her she waits and calls from where she is; at the end she lowers her face to you and closes her eyes when you touch her nose.
5. Tomorrow: standing on a needle above the clouds while a glowing dragon circles you, and her giant face stopping right in front of you.
Emotion: thrill of height -> awe (w01 fear, w02 cheer, w03 calm wonder in the deep, w04 playful fear).
