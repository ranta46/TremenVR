# w03 plan — The Whale Below (world: the Hollow Sea)

## Idea A (rejected): "Paper Lantern Market"
Walk a night market and tap paper lanterns to collect stamps for a fox shopkeeper.
Test 1: would it be as fun as a flat phone game? -> YES (it is a tapping/collecting list). REJECTED.

## Idea B (rejected): "The Giant's Palm"
You stand on the palm of a sleeping stone giant; keep birds off its face so it does not wake.
Strong scale, but the verb is "tap the birds" again. Weak on body action. REJECTED.

## Idea C (chosen): "The Whale Below"
1. You sit inside a small glass diving bell, sinking into a dark, endless sea trench.
2. A whale the size of a ship glides right over the bell from behind you. Her name is Oona; her lights went out and she is lost.
3. Bix, a tiny lantern fish, swims at the glass and follows your eyes. Your lamp shines wherever you look.
4. Turn toward Oona's song (it comes from the side, from behind, from below) and hold your light on her so she can see.
5. When our lamp dies, Oona's giant eye opens right at the glass. Hold still and let her see you. She lights up the whole sea and carries you to the sunrise.

## The test
1. Flat phone game? No. It is about turning your body toward a sound and the scale of a whale passing over your head; on a flat screen it is only a picture.
2. Body action: turn to face the song (spatial sound), hold your gaze, look up, look down, and in the climax hold completely still.
3. Whoa (0-15 s): a deep song BEHIND you, then a whale 40 m long passes a few meters over the glass. Bix: "Look up!"
4. Reaction: Bix swims into your view and wiggles when you look at him. Oona follows your light, and in the dark her eye looks right at you; if you look away she drifts back.
5. Tomorrow: a whale eye bigger than you, right at the glass, looking at you in total darkness; then the whole sea lighting up blue.
Emotion: wonder and warmth (w01 was fear, w02 cheerful).
