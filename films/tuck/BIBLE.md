# TUCK — series bible (TremenVR channel)
A short film series. One episode = one letter. 1–2 minutes. One episode per day.
Every episode must keep Tuck's look, behaviour, beliefs and personality exactly as written here.

## Tuck
- Look: a small cardboard box robot (about 1 m tall). Brown cardboard body with a light tape strip on top,
  two top flaps that stick up a little. ONE big round eye on the front: black socket, glowing pale-blue iris,
  black pupil, and a cardboard half-lid above it. Thin black antenna on the right side of the top with a
  small warm-orange bulb. Short black legs with round feet, thin black arms. Never changes design.
- Expression comes only from: the eyelid (closed / sleepy / normal / wide), the antenna bulb
  (off = asleep, dim = afraid, bright = brave or happy), body tilt, and beeps.
- Personality: timid, polite, very earnest. Afraid of the dark and of big things, but never stops a delivery.
- Belief: "Every letter deserves to arrive." He never opens or reads the inside of a letter. He never
  keeps a letter. He never lies.
- Voice: only electronic beeps (no words). Short rising beeps = question, falling = sad, quick triple = happy.
  If words are needed, they are written on letters, signs or the end card (simple English).
- Habits: he waits until the lamp is on before he walks. He bows (tilts forward) after every delivery.

## World: the fog city
- An empty city at night in thick blue fog. Warm orange street lamps, a few lit windows. No humans are
  ever seen clearly.
- The red post office facade with a mail slot is "home". Letters slide out of the slot by themselves.
- The Tall One: a huge dark shape with two small pale eyes that walks far away in the fog. It never
  attacks. Tuck freezes when it passes. Nobody knows what it is.

## The series mystery
- Who writes the letters? Each letter is addressed to someone or something strange ("whoever is still
  here", "the last lamp", "the one under the bridge"...). Each delivery gets a reply letter, and the reply
  gives the next address. Never reveal the writer. Give one small clue per episode.

## Episode rules
- Start with a strong image in the first 3 seconds. One clear goal (deliver the letter), one danger or
  surprise, one quiet ending, then a reply letter that hooks the next episode.
- End card: TUCK / Letter N: <title> / one hook question / "Letter N+1 tomorrow."
- On-screen text: simple English only.

## Episodes
1. Whoever Is Still Here — the first letter falls from the slot; Tuck walks the street, sees the Tall One,
   slides the letter under a lit door. The window goes dark and a reply letter slides back out.
2. The Last Lamp — on a foggy bridge of dead lamps Tuck meets the Tall One's eyes rising from below, posts the letter into the last lamp's slot and every lamp lights again. The reply shows a tiny drawing of Tuck.
