# w04 plan — The Moss Giants (world: Mossmere)

## Idea A (rejected): "Lantern Boat Race"
Steer a paper boat down a river by tilting; collect lantern lights.
Test 1: as fun as a flat phone game? -> YES (steering + collecting). REJECTED.

## Idea B (rejected): "The Giant's Palm"
A gentle cloud giant lifts you up on her palm to hang the moon. Look down, feel the height.
Strong scale, but almost no play after the first look. Weak verb. REJECTED.

## Idea C (chosen): "The Moss Giants" (the statue game in 360 degrees)
1. Night, a moss clearing in a huge cedar forest under a giant moon. Nib, a tiny paper-lantern spirit, sits by your feet.
2. A ring of mossy stone giants stands around you. They only move when NOBODY looks at them.
3. Turn your head to freeze them. Grinding stone behind you tells you one is creeping up. If one reaches you, it taps your lantern.
4. More and faster, from all sides, until dawn. Every time you turn back they are in a new silly pose.
5. Climax: the ground shakes, Gran Mossa (25 m tall) rises BEHIND you and reaches down. Look up into her eyes and hold your gaze. The sun rises; flowers open on every stone; they only wanted someone to play with.

## The test
1. Flat phone game? No. The danger is always where you are NOT looking: behind you, at your side. On a flat screen you would just see one direction; here you must spin your body to the sound.
2. Body action: look around / turn to face the sound / look behind you, then look UP and hold your gaze.
3. Whoa (0-15 s): grinding stone behind you; you turn and a 3 m stone face is bent down right in front of your nose, eyes glowing.
4. Reaction: every giant faces you, freezes the moment you look, changes pose when you don't; Nib hides behind your view and peeks out when you look at him; Gran Mossa leans in only while you look away, and smiles when you hold her gaze.
5. Tomorrow: turning around and a huge mossy face is right there; then looking up at a giant as tall as the trees, sunrise behind her.
Emotion: surprise (playful fear -> warmth). w01 fear, w02 cheer, w03 wonder.
