# Characters used in TremenVR works
Rule: a character that comes back must keep the same look, behavior, beliefs and personality.
Add every new character here (look, personality, beliefs, way of speaking, which works).

## Rook (w01 The Signal Tower)
Small train conductor of Kesmoor. Brave but nervous, speaks in short calm orders ("Now! All of them!").
Believes the last train must always come home. Never runs away from the Hush.
## The Hush (w01)
Huge silent shape that eats light. Never speaks. Snuffs lamps. Freezes the player with a stare;
it leaves if you don't move. Not evil, just hungry for light. Always comes back.
## Pip (w02 Pip's Star Jar)
Small orange star-fox of the Skyfold (floating sky islands). Round body, white belly and muzzle, big black shiny eyes,
pink inner ears and cheeks, a little gold star on the forehead, blue scarf, big fluffy tail with a white tip. Keeps a glass star jar.
Cheerful, quick, a bit of a show-off but kind; gets scared, then brave. Believes every falling star is someone's wish
and must be caught before it breaks, then given back to the sky. Speaks fast and bouncy, short lines, counts out loud
("One! Ooh, it's warm.", "Six! You're good at this!"), says "Uh-oh" and "Whoa!".
## Grumble (w02)
The big comet. Huge round glowing ball of pale rock and ice with a long warm golden tail, heavy dark eyebrows,
a small round mouth. Not evil: very old, very sleepy, falls asleep while flying and drifts down (snores "Zzz").
Wakes up when tapped, is sorry and leaves a shower of stars as a gift. Speaks slow and deep, few words
("Hm? ...Oh! Was I falling? Sorry, little one.").
## Oona (w03 The Whale Below)
A giant old whale of the Hollow Sea, about 40 m long (the size of a ship). Slate-blue back, pale grooved belly, long
side fins, wide tail. One huge kind eye on each side: pale white, deep blue iris, a soft blue glow. Hundreds of small
blue light-spots along her body; when they are dark she cannot find her way. Gentle, slow, patient, a little shy.
Believes every small light in the sea is a friend. Never speaks in words: she sings long low songs that slide up and down;
a happy song is a warm chord. Looks right at you with one eye, leans in when you are still, drifts back if you move fast.
## Bix (w03)
A tiny round lantern fish, blue-teal with a pale belly, big round white eyes with black pupils, small smile, orange fins and tail,
and a little warm-yellow light on a curved stalk over his head. Nervous but loyal, talks a lot when scared, gets brave for friends.
Believes "a small light is still a light." Speaks in short, quick, excited lines ("Look up! LOOK UP!", "Shh... Hear that?"),
little squeaky blips when he talks. Swims at the glass and follows your eyes; wiggles and spins when you look at him.
## Nib (w04 The Moss Giants)
A tiny paper-lantern spirit of Mossmere, about the size of a pear. Round warm-yellow paper body with dark red ribs,
brown caps on top and bottom, two small black eyes, pink cheeks, a little smile, thin stick legs and one green leaf on top.
Glows warm; the glow dims when a giant pokes him. Small, jumpy and a bit dramatic ("Eek!"), but loyal and brave.
Believes "every game is fun if you play it together." Speaks in short whispers and squeaks ("Shh...", "Behind you!",
"Eek! Tag!"), little bell-like blips when he talks. Stays near your feet, follows your eyes and wiggles when you look at him.
## The Moss Giants (w04)
Round stone giants about 3 m tall, grey stone with thick green moss on their heads, shoulders and hands. Heavy brow,
big round nose, two glowing amber eyes, a small round "o" mouth (a wide smile when happy). Stubby legs, long heavy arms.
Playful, like big children. They only move when nobody is looking at them (the stone game), and freeze in silly poses
the moment you look. Never speak: they hop with heavy thuds, grind like stone, and laugh "ho ho ho". Not evil:
they just want someone to play with until morning. At sunrise small flowers open on their moss and they sleep as stones.
## Gran Mossa (w04)
The oldest Moss Giant, as tall as the cedar trees (about 26 m). Same look as the Moss Giants but huge, with long moss
hair hanging around her face. Slow, kind, a little mischievous. Rises from the ground at the end of the night.
Leans down and reaches for you only while you look away; if you hold her gaze, she smiles and the sun comes up behind her.
Never speaks; laughs very deep and slow. Believes the night game ends only when someone is brave enough to look her in the eye.
